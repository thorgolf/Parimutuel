RPGC Club Championship Parimutuel

In each flight, bet exactly $10 total, split however you like across one or more golfers. Winnings from each flight's pool are paid out to whoever bet on the winning golfer, in proportion to their stake. For example, if you bet $10 on Joe Blow and he wins, if there was $100 bet on Joe Blow you would win $10 (10%). If nobody picks a flight winner correctly, that flight's money will be split evenly between the other flights.
